Watching Debates

Spot these while they're happening, not after:

Gish gallopMore claims than anyone could fact-check in the time given
StrawmanningRestating the other position in its weakest form, then attacking that
Motte-and-baileyDefending a modest claim while implying a much bigger one
False dichotomyOnly two options offered when more actually exist
